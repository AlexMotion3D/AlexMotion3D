About Me

I turn products into motion that people remember.

Hi, I'm Alex — a 3D product animation artist. I create sleek, cinematic renders and animations for beauty, tech and lifestyle brands, from the first sketch of an idea to the final polished frame.

Every project starts with the product itself: its shape, its materials, the way light touches it. I build clean, premium visuals that make products feel desirable and ready for ads, social media and launches.

I've had the pleasure of working on projects for brands such as Estée Lauder, Insta360 and Dior, as well as my own concept work.
